# DAOVOS Company Profile

> [!abstract] What DAOVOS is
> Digital agency / web design & development studio. Starts with websites; built from day one as a broader digital brand able to expand into branding, creative services, digital products, e-commerce, automation, software, and other tech-driven services.
> Related: [[DAOVOS Site]] · [[DAOVOS Design System]]

## Mission

Make high-quality digital experiences accessible to people and businesses by combining strong design, modern technology, and a simple client experience.

## Vision

Grow Daovos from a web development agency into a recognizable digital company that can provide everything a modern brand needs to build, launch, and grow online.

## Core Services (now)

- Custom website design
- Website development
- Landing pages
- Portfolio websites
- Business websites
- E-commerce websites
- Website redesigns
- Responsive / mobile optimization
- Website maintenance
- UI/UX design

## Future Services (roadmap)

Brand identity · Logo & visual systems · Creative direction · Web applications · SaaS development · AI & automation · Digital marketing · SEO · Content creation · Hosting & managed infrastructure · Digital templates & products.

## Target Clients

Startups · Small & medium businesses · Creators · Personal brands · Online businesses · Agencies needing development support · Entrepreneurs launching new ideas.

## Brand Personality

Modern. Precise. Ambitious. Creative. **Premium without being inaccessible.** Feels like a digital studio / technology company — not a traditional corporate agency.

> [!tip] Tone calibration for copywriting
> Confident and engineered (matches the [[DAOVOS Design System]] monolith aesthetic) but welcoming — avoid cold corporate jargon AND avoid playful startup slang.

## Copy Context Note (for agents)

Current hero copy (`HeroBottomLeft`, `HeroAccentOverlay`) speaks abstractly — "ARCHITECTURAL DIGITAL SYSTEMS", "modular visual operating systems", "Engineered for scale." That's the aspirational studio voice layered over a concrete web-agency offer. When writing client-facing copy (services, CTAs), ground it in the Core Services list above; keep the monumental voice for atmosphere.
